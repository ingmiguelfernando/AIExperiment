# Contract — Fernhill Tours and KiwiAir (KiwiFlex)

**Agreement reference:** KA-CON-2024-017
**Parties:** KiwiAir Holdings Limited and Fernhill Tours Limited
**Effective date:** 1 April 2024
**Term:** 3 years, expiring 31 March 2027

## Background

Fernhill Tours operates guided multi-day walking tours across the South Island, carrying approximately 4,200 guests per year. Tour itineraries frequently change due to weather, which makes fare flexibility the central commercial requirement.

## Scope

KiwiAir provides Fernhill Tours with discounted access to the KiwiFlex product across the KiwiAir network, with particular volume on the Queenstown, Christchurch and Wānaka routes.

## Commercial terms

- **KiwiFlex supplement:** discounted to a flat NZD 29 per sector regardless of route length, against the standard range of NZD 39 to NZD 79.
- **Minimum volume:** 3,000 sectors per contract year. Fernhill Tours booked 3,840 sectors in year one and 4,110 in year two.
- **Payment terms:** 30 days from invoice, monthly consolidated billing.
- **Group bookings:** parties of 10 or more receive an additional 8 percent discount on the base fare.

## Weather cancellation clause

Where a tour is cancelled due to a Civil Defence warning or a Department of Conservation track closure, KiwiAir will refund the full fare to the original payment method rather than issuing travel credit. This clause was the primary reason Fernhill Tours selected KiwiAir over the incumbent carrier.

This clause has been invoked 14 times, totalling NZD 61,200 in refunds.

## Service commitments

- Confirmed seat allocation released no later than 48 hours before departure
- Named account contact with a two-business-day response commitment
- Quarterly business review

## Renewal

The agreement contains a right of first refusal for Fernhill Tours on renewal terms. Renewal discussions are scheduled to begin in October 2026.

Account owner at KiwiAir is the Commercial team, with escalation to Priya Raman for service issues.
