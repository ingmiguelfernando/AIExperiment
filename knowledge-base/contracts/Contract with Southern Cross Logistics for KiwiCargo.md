# Contract — Southern Cross Logistics and KiwiAir (KiwiCargo)

**Agreement reference:** KA-CON-2023-004
**Parties:** KiwiAir Holdings Limited and Southern Cross Logistics Limited
**Effective date:** 1 July 2023
**Term:** 5 years, expiring 30 June 2028

## Background

Southern Cross Logistics is a freight forwarder specialising in seafood exports from Nelson and Bluff. It is KiwiAir's largest single customer by revenue.

## Scope

Southern Cross Logistics has guaranteed capacity on KiwiCargo dedicated freight rotations, primarily the overnight Nelson to Auckland and Bluff to Christchurch services.

## Commercial terms

- **Guaranteed capacity:** 4,000 kg per night across the network, Monday to Saturday.
- **Rate:** NZD 3.15 per kg for KiwiCargo Overnight, against the published rate of NZD 4.20.
- **Volume above guarantee:** charged at NZD 3.70 per kg, subject to availability.
- **Annual minimum spend:** NZD 6.2 million. Actual spend was NZD 7.9 million in FY2025 and NZD 9.4 million in FY2026.
- **Payment terms:** 14 days from invoice.

## Cold chain commitment

All perishable consignments are carried in temperature-controlled containers maintained between 0 and 4 degrees Celsius, with continuous temperature logging. Logs are provided to Southern Cross Logistics within two hours of delivery.

Where a cold chain breach is recorded, KiwiAir credits 150 percent of the freight charge for the affected consignment. Three breaches have occurred since inception, totalling NZD 28,400 in credits.

## Performance commitments

| Metric | Commitment | FY2026 actual |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 93 percent | 95.2 percent |
| Cold chain integrity | 99.5 percent | 99.8 percent |
| Capacity honoured | 98 percent | 99.1 percent |

Failure to meet on-time delivery for two consecutive quarters triggers a rate review in Southern Cross Logistics' favour. This has not occurred.

## Exclusivity

Southern Cross Logistics is not exclusive to KiwiAir, but KiwiAir has agreed not to offer rates below NZD 3.15 per kg to any competing freight forwarder on the Nelson and Bluff routes for the duration of the term.

## Renewal

Automatic 2-year extension unless either party gives 12 months notice. Notice window opens 1 July 2027.
