# Product — KiwiFlex

## What it is

KiwiFlex is KiwiAir's flexible fare product for travellers whose plans change. It was launched in November 2021 and is the airline's highest-margin passenger product.

## How it works

A KiwiFlex fare can be changed an unlimited number of times up to two hours before departure, with no change fee. If the new fare is cheaper, the difference is refunded to the original payment method rather than held as credit.

Unlike most flexible fares in the market, KiwiFlex does not require the new booking to be in the same name. A KiwiFlex ticket can be transferred to another traveller once, free of charge.

## Pricing

KiwiFlex is priced as a supplement over the base fare:

| Route length | Supplement (NZD) |
|---|---|
| Under 250 km | 39 |
| 250 to 500 km | 59 |
| Over 500 km | 79 |

The supplement is flat and does not scale with the base fare. A KiwiFlex supplement purchased on a NZD 89 fare costs the same as one purchased on a NZD 340 fare.

## What is included

- Unlimited date and time changes up to two hours before departure
- One free transfer to another traveller
- 23 kg checked baggage (the standard KiwiAir allowance, included in all fares)
- Priority standby if a flight is disrupted
- Seat selection at no charge
- Full refund to original payment method if cancelled more than 24 hours before departure

## What is not included

- Refunds within 24 hours of departure, which are issued as travel credit valid for 12 months
- Lounge access, which KiwiAir does not offer at any airport
- Guaranteed rebooking onto other carriers

## Performance

KiwiFlex attach rate reached 22 percent of all bookings in FY2026, generating NZD 31.4 million in supplement revenue. Corporate travellers account for 61 percent of KiwiFlex purchases.

Priya Raman's customer experience team reports that KiwiFlex bookings generate 74 percent fewer complaints per passenger than standard fares.
