# Product — SkyPoints

## What it is

SkyPoints is KiwiAir's loyalty programme, launched in August 2023. It reached 100,000 members within six months and had 412,000 members by the end of FY2026.

## How points are earned

Members earn 1 SkyPoint per NZD 1 spent on KiwiAir fares, including the KiwiFlex supplement. Points are earned on the fare paid, not on distance flown, which means short regional routes earn proportionally more than distance-based schemes.

KiwiCargo customers earn 1 SkyPoint per NZD 2 spent.

## How points are redeemed

SkyPoints redeem against fares at a fixed rate of **100 points equals NZD 1**. There are no blackout dates, no seat caps, and no separate reward inventory. Any seat available for sale can be bought with points.

Partial redemption is allowed from 500 points. Members can pay any combination of points and cash.

## Tiers

| Tier | Qualification (points earned in 12 months) | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Member | 0 | Standard earning |
| Kea | 15,000 | 25 percent bonus earning, priority standby |
| Kākā | 40,000 | 50 percent bonus earning, free KiwiFlex on all bookings |
| Kākāpō | 90,000 | 75 percent bonus earning, free KiwiFlex, two guest upgrades per year |

Tier status is held for 12 months from the date of qualification, not by calendar year.

## Expiry

SkyPoints expire after 36 months of account inactivity. Any earning or redemption activity resets the clock on the entire balance. KiwiAir does not expire points on a rolling per-point basis, a decision Priya Raman's team advocated for on the grounds that rolling expiry is the single largest driver of loyalty programme complaints across the industry.

## Performance

SkyPoints members fly 2.6 times more often than non-members. The programme's breakage rate, meaning points earned but never redeemed, is 14 percent, which is deliberately low by industry standards.

In FY2026, 19 percent of all KiwiAir bookings involved at least a partial points redemption.
