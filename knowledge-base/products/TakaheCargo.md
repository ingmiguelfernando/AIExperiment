# Product — TakaheCargo

## What it is

TakaheCargo is Takahe Air's air freight service, operating on the same network as the passenger business. It launched in April 2021 during the pandemic downturn, when the airline converted two aircraft to freight configuration to replace lost passenger revenue.

## How it works

TakaheCargo uses two models:

**Belly freight.** Cargo carried in the hold of scheduled passenger flights. Available on all fifteen destinations, with capacity varying by passenger load.

**Dedicated freight.** Two ATR 72-600 aircraft operate in full freight configuration on overnight rotations between Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch, returning to passenger configuration for daytime flying. The conversion takes 90 minutes.

## Service levels

| Service | Delivery commitment | Price (per kg, NZD) |
|---|---|---|
| TakaheCargo Same Day | Airport to airport within 6 hours | 8.90 |
| TakaheCargo Overnight | Delivered by 10am next business day | 4.20 |
| TakaheCargo Standard | 2 to 3 business days | 2.15 |

Minimum charge is NZD 45 per consignment across all service levels.

## Specialist handling

TakaheCargo is certified for:

- **Perishables.** Temperature-controlled containers for seafood and horticulture, the largest category by volume.
- **Pharmaceutical.** Cold chain from 2 to 8 degrees Celsius with continuous logging.
- **Dangerous goods.** Class 3, 8 and 9 only. Takahe Air does not carry Class 1 explosives or Class 7 radioactive material.
- **Live animals.** Domestic pets and day-old chicks. No livestock.

## Performance

TakaheCargo generated NZD 67.3 million in FY2026, representing 31 percent of total airline revenue. Seafood exports from Nelson and Bluff account for 38 percent of TakaheCargo tonnage.

On-time delivery performance was 94.1 percent in FY2026, against a published target of 92 percent.

## Notes

The decision to launch TakaheCargo is credited with keeping Takahe Air solvent through 2021. The airline retains the dual-configuration capability deliberately, even though it adds maintenance complexity, because it provides a hedge against passenger demand shocks.
